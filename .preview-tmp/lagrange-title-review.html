How this was produced. Each title was checked against the PEN America Index of School Book Bans for 2021 through 2025, American Library Association challenge records, documented Moms for Liberty challenges, and district and news records. Sources that cannot document a district action, including award pages, retail listings, social posts, study guides and library catalog entries, were excluded. Status wording mirrors what each source recorded.

About the state labels. A state appears on a title when a district in that state has a documented action on record. Historical and out-of-state actions are labeled as such, and are not the same as an action in your district.

Please read this part. This review was performed by hand, title by title, by the Bookmarked team. OnShelf does not produce this automatically yet. The engineering work to bring the platform to this standard is specified and underway. We would rather tell you where that line sits than let it be assumed.

Bookmarked informs. Districts decide. If anything here does not match your records, tell us and we will re-check it.
