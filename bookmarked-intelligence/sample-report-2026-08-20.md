## Method + provenance

Two channels per title (spec §2): known-list check (PEN America, ALA OIF, Take Back the Classroom,
Moms for Liberty lineage, Moms for America, state lists), then the content-evidence prompt over
admissible sources only — professional review journals, state/district records, staff-reported news,
advocacy rationale documents. UGC (social media, forums, reader reviews), retail/resale listings, and
Common Sense Media are excluded by rule. Every claim distinguishes *content description* from
*challenge record* — a challenge somewhere is not proof of content. The QC sweep (`qc-sweep.py`)
verified zero blocklisted sources and exact no-evidence language across all cards.
