**This skill contains no color values, no font names, and no approved phrases — deliberately.** Those live in 174981121 and change there. If you find yourself about to hardcode a hex value into collateral, load the canon instead.

**If any other skill, archived doc, or template conflicts with 174981121, 174981121 wins.** Several superseded "brand system" documents are still in circulation carrying a retired palette. Flag them when you find them.

## STEP 1 — LOAD CANON

Read Confluence 174981121 first, every time. Do not proceed from memory of what the brand looks like. The palette and font were both changed in July 2026, and older values are on an explicit flag-on-sight list.

## STEP 2 — ESTABLISH THE BRIEF

Before designing, settle four things:

1. **Audience** — superintendent, librarian, board, ESC partner, investor. This drives voice mode.
2. **Moment** — is this handed over in a meeting, emailed cold, or presented live?
3. **The single takeaway** — one sentence. If there are two, it is two pieces of collateral.
4. **Format and length** — and who has to physically use it.

## STEP 3 — VOICE MODE

One mode per piece, never mixed. **Trusted Guide** is the default for roughly 90% of work. Visionary Authority is boards and investors only. Practical Partner is customer ops. Clear Operator is product and release notes. Canon defines each — load it.

## STEP 4 — STRUCTURE

**Deck** — lead with the customer's problem, not the company. Establish the operational reality, then the consequence, then Bookmarked as the resolution. One idea per slide. If a slide needs a paragraph, it is a document, not a slide.

**One-pager** — headline states the outcome; the body proves it; the footer says what to do next. It must survive being read in ninety seconds by someone who is not paying full attention.

## STEP 5 — QUALITY GATE (run before handing anything back)

- [ ] Loaded 174981121 in this session — not working from memory
- [ ] Every color and the font come from current canon; no retired values present
- [ ] Real logo file used — never the word "Bookmarked" typed as a logo
- [ ] Light background (marketing rule)
- [ ] One voice mode, consistently
- [ ] No never-say phrases — including specific cost/time figures and multi-state claims
- [ ] Texas-only framing maintained
- [ ] No invented product features; anything about the product traced to a source
- [ ] No real district, contact, or student names unless the piece is explicitly for that district
- [ ] Single takeaway is unmistakable

## VERIFY BEFORE SHOWING

Render the artifact and look at it before presenting it. Check that text is not clipped, contrast holds, the logo is not distorted, and the page breaks land where intended. Do not describe a deliverable you have not viewed.
